pagenorma started as a spreadsheet that never quite fit a literary translation desk. It knew how many pages were left, but it could not tell me what that meant for this week's income or whether a deadline was quietly slipping.

This blog is where we'll write about the things that came out of that gap: what changes in the app, how we think about pacing and QA, and what "local-first" means in practice.

## What to expect

- **Product updates** — short notes on what shipped and why.
- **Workflow notes** — pacing, deadlines and keeping prose consistent across a long manuscript.
- **Privacy and data** — plain-language explanations of storage and AI processing.

Nothing here is a substitute for the [support guides](/support/). Those are the reference manual; this is the workshop notebook.
