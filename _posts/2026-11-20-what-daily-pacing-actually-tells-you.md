A daily pace number is easy to misread as a grade. On a literary project, it is much more useful as a weather report: it tells you what kind of week you are walking into.

## Read the trend, not the day

One slow day says very little. A target that keeps drifting upward while pages stay constant says that the deadline is getting tighter than the manuscript allows. pagenorma shows the required pace next to your actual pace so the gap is visible before it becomes a crisis.

## Pair pace with earnings

Pacing without pay is only half the picture. When you can see the net amount attached to the remaining pages, it becomes much easier to decide whether to protect a slot for another client or push through the current one.

That is the small idea behind the dashboard: not to make you type faster, but to make the trade-offs visible while there is still time to choose.
