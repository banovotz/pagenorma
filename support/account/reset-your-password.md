## Request a reset link

1. Open the menu and choose **Login**.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Click **Forgot password?**
4. Click **Send reset link**.

pagenorma shows a message saying that if an account exists for this address, a reset link has been sent. It says this whether or not the address has an account.

## Set a new password

1. Open the link in the email. It takes you to the pagenorma login page.
2. Enter your new password under **Set new password** and click **Save new password**.
3. When you see *Password successfully updated*, sign in with the new password.

## Signed up with Google or Hugging Face?

If you always sign in with **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Hugging Face**, you may not have a pagenorma password. Use that button to sign in.
