You can sign in to pagenorma in three ways:

- **Google** — click **Continue with Google**.
- **Hugging Face** — click **Continue with Hugging Face**.
- **Email and password** — enter your email and password and click **Sign in**.

They all open the same account. Your token balance and allowance are shared, whichever method you use.

## See which methods are connected

Open **Profile**. The **Sign-in methods** card lists the methods linked to your account.

## Add Hugging Face to an existing account

If you are signed in and connect Hugging Face from **Settings**, it is added as another sign-in method. See [Connect Hugging Face]({% link support/connections/connect-hugging-face.md %}).

## If sign-in does not work

- Allow pop-ups for pagenorma. Google and Hugging Face open in a pop-up window.
- Forgot your password? See [Reset your password]({% link support/account/reset-your-password.md %}).
- For error messages, see [Error messages explained]({% link support/troubleshooting/error-messages-explained.md %}).
