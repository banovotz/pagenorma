Your email address must be verified before you can use AI analysis or view your monthly allowance. Accounts created with Google are normally verified by Google already.

## What you will see

Until your address is verified, the **Profile** page shows a notice: *Your email must be verified before you can use AI analysis or view its monthly allowance.*

## Verify your address

1. Open the verification email from pagenorma and click the link in it. If you cannot find it, check your spam folder.
2. Go back to pagenorma, open **Profile** and click **I have verified my email**. The notice disappears once your status refreshes.

## No email arrived?

On the **Profile** page, click **Resend verification email**.

## Why is this required?

The AI features use a shared allowance. Verification makes sure each allowance belongs to a real, reachable email address.
