You do not need an account to track projects, but you need one for the AI features (analysis, glossary and thesaurus), for your monthly allowance and for tokens.

## Register with email and password

1. Open the menu and choose **Login**.
2. Click **New account**.
3. Enter your **Email** and a password of at least six characters.
4. Tick **I accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy**. You cannot create an account without accepting them.
5. Optionally tick the box to receive promotional offers and marketing communications. You can change this later on your [profile page]({% link support/account/your-profile-page.md %}).
6. Click **Create account**.

pagenorma sends a verification email. You must [verify your email]({% link support/account/verify-your-email.md %}) before you can start an analysis.

## Register with Google or Hugging Face

On the login page, click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Hugging Face** and follow the pop-up window. Your browser may ask you to allow pop-ups.

## One account, several ways in

All sign-in methods open the same account. See [Sign-in methods]({% link support/account/sign-in-methods.md %}).
