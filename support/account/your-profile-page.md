Once you are signed in, the **Login** menu item becomes **Profile**.

## What is on the page

- **Signed in as** — the email address of your account.
- **Verification notice** — shown until your email is verified. See [Verify your email address]({% link support/account/verify-your-email.md %}).
- **Analysis tokens** — your token balance, plus a box to redeem a voucher code. One token covers 100 standard analysis pages, and your free monthly allowance is always used first. See [Tokens explained]({% link support/usage/tokens-explained.md %}).
- **Marketing preferences** — a checkbox for promotional offers and marketing communications. Tick or untick it at any time.
- **Sign-in methods** — which methods open your account.
- **Sign out** and **Delete account**.

## Sign out

Click **Sign out**. Your projects stay in the browser; only your account session ends.
