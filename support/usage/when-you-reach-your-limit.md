## What you may see

- Before a run, if the pages you selected are more than your remaining budget, pagenorma refuses to start and suggests **Fit to allowance**.
- During a run, if your free pages and tokens only cover part of the text, the analysis covers the first part and a notice says, for example, that only the first N of M standard pages were analysed because your allowance and tokens covered that many.
- For the glossary, a matching notice says the glossary covers only the first pages.
- *Your glossary coverage allowance has been used up.* — you have no glossary pages left.
- In the thesaurus: *Not enough tokens are left for the thesaurus.*

The notice also tells you the date when free pages unlock again.

## Your options

1. **Wait for the reset.** Your allowance renews on the first day of the month.
2. **Choose fewer pages.** Use [page ranges and Fit to allowance]({% link support/analysis/choose-pages-to-analyse.md %}).
3. **Redeem a voucher** if you have one. See [Redeem a voucher]({% link support/usage/redeem-a-voucher.md %}).
4. **Buy tokens** — coming soon. See [Tokens explained]({% link support/usage/tokens-explained.md %}).
5. **Use pagenorma QS**, which has no monthly limits. See [pagenorma S and pagenorma QS]({% link support/getting-started/plans-pagenorma-s-and-qs.md %}).

## Partial analyses are still useful

A partial result is saved and can be opened as usual. The side-by-side view shows a notice that it covers only some pages, and you can analyse the remaining pages later.
