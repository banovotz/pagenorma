Tokens extend your use of the AI features beyond the free monthly allowance on pagenorma S.

## What a token buys

**1 token =**

- **100** standard analysis pages, or
- glossary for **10** 10 standard pages, or
- **500** thesaurus searches.

## How tokens are spent

1. Your free monthly allowance is always used first.
2. Then your tokens are used.

Tokens are added in whole units but used page by page. That is why a balance can show a fraction, such as 9.95 tokens.

## Tokens do not expire

Unused tokens stay on your account. They are shared by analysis, glossary and thesaurus.

## Where to see your balance

On the Dashboard allowance card and on your [profile page]({% link support/account/your-profile-page.md %}), for example *9.95 tokens (995 token pages)*.

## Getting tokens

- **Vouchers** — redeem a code. See [Redeem a voucher]({% link support/usage/redeem-a-voucher.md %}).
- **Buying** — not available yet. The Dashboard shows a **Buy tokens** panel marked *Coming soon* with preview prices: 10 tokens for €9, 20 for €16 and 100 for €49. These prices are for display only until purchasing opens.

pagenorma QS does not use tokens.
