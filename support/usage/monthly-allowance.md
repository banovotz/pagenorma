On pagenorma S you get a free allowance of AI use every month.

## What is included

| Allowance | Free per month |
|---|---|
| **Analysis pages** (commentary) | 5 standard pages |
| **Glossary pages** | 5 standard pages |
| **Thesaurus searches** | 100 searches |

Analysis pages and glossary pages are counted separately. A glossary-only run spends glossary pages and leaves your analysis pages untouched.

## When it resets

The allowance resets on the **first day of each month**, using UTC (Coordinated Universal Time). The Dashboard card shows the exact date: *Resets on …*

## See what is left

The **AI analysis allowance** card at the top of the Dashboard shows three counters, such as *3 / 5 analysis pages*, *5 / 5 glossary pages* and *80 / 100 thesaurus searches*. You must be signed in with a verified email to see it.

## How pages are counted

- A page is 1800 characters with spaces. See [Standard pages explained]({% link support/projects/standard-pages-explained.md %}).
- A run is charged for the larger of the source and translation text, rounded up to whole pages, or for the pages you selected.
- Pages are recorded after a phase finishes, not when it starts.
- Your usage is tracked on pagenorma's servers, so clearing your browser or switching device does not give you a fresh allowance.

## Need more?

Your free allowance is always used first, then your [tokens]({% link support/usage/tokens-explained.md %}). pagenorma QS has no monthly limits.
