Because translations are rarely exactly as long as the source, the progress bar may stop just short of 100% even when you have finished.

## Confirm completion

When your translation reaches 95% of the source by character count, the card shows a note such as *Translation is 97% by character count. If it is complete, confirm it to show 100%.*

1. Click **Mark as completed**.
2. Confirm the message. The card then shows 100% regardless of character-count differences.

## Reopen a project

A completed project shows *Completed manually confirmed* with a **Reopen project** button. Click it if you need to carry on working, and progress goes back to being calculated from your text.

## Good to know

Marking a project complete changes how the dashboard shows progress. It does not change the date or the amount your earnings count for in [Financial Analytics]({% link support/earnings/financial-analytics.md %}).
