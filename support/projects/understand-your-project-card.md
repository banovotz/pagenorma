Each project on the Dashboard has a card. Here is what it shows.

## Progress

**Progress: 120.50 / 220.00 pages (55%)** compares the pages you have translated with the pages in the book.

- Translated pages are counted from the characters of your translation, at 1800 characters per standard page.
- The total is the source page count. If your translation grows longer than the source, the total follows it, so the bar never goes past 100%.
- A small line shows the raw numbers: characters in the source and in the translation, with their page equivalents. A note appears when the translation is longer, because untranslated front and back matter and differences between languages can make the counts differ.

## Pace

- **Planned pace** is your **Daily Target** from the project settings, or *Not set*.
- **Required pace** is the remaining pages divided by the days left until the deadline. It counts business days, or all days if you work weekends. It is shown in **red** when you have set a planned pace and the required pace is higher, meaning you are behind your own plan, and in teal otherwise.
- After the deadline the card says **Deadline has passed!** When the project is complete it says **Project completed!**

## Earnings

**Earnings: €1,506.25 / €2,750.00** is what you have earned so far against the total. Both are based on translated pages multiplied by your rate per page, and the rate is shown next to it.

## Buttons

- **Edit** — change the project. See [Edit or delete a project]({% link support/projects/edit-or-delete-a-project.md %}).
- **Refresh Translation** — re-read your translation. See [Refresh your translation]({% link support/projects/refresh-translation.md %}).
- **Analyze Text** — start an AI analysis. See [Run an analysis]({% link support/analysis/run-an-analysis.md %}).
- **Delete** — remove the project.

## The allowance card

At the top of the Dashboard, the *AI analysis allowance* card shows what is left of your monthly allowance. See [Monthly allowance]({% link support/usage/monthly-allowance.md %}).
