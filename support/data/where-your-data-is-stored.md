pagenorma does not keep your projects on a server. Most of your work lives in your own browser.

## Stored in your browser, on your device

- **Projects** — their settings, the text read from your source and translation, and the cover image.
- **Saved analyses and glossaries.**
- **Deduction settings** from Settings.
- **Your Hugging Face token**, if you connected one.

## Stored in your pagenorma account

- Your email address and sign-in methods.
- Your token balance and your monthly usage.
- Your marketing and consent choices.

## Not stored by pagenorma

Your Google access token is kept only for the current browser session. Your Google Docs stay in Google.

## What this means

- **A different browser or device starts empty.** To move your work, use [backup and restore]({% link support/data/back-up-and-restore.md %}).
- **Clearing site data removes your projects.** pagenorma asks your browser to keep its storage safe from automatic clean-up, but a browser can still refuse, or lose data if you clear it yourself.
- **Private or incognito windows** do not keep data after they close.
- Your allowance and tokens belong to your account, so they follow you to any device.

Make a [backup]({% link support/data/back-up-and-restore.md %}) regularly, especially before clearing your browser or changing devices.
