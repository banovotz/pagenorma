The **Thesaurus** screen finds synonyms for a word, grouped by part of speech. It is powered by AI, so it can handle words in context and across languages.

## Search

1. Open **Thesaurus** from the menu.
2. Type a word in **Search term**.
3. Choose the **Language**.
4. Click **Search**.

Languages: English, Croatian, Serbian, Slovenian, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese, Swedish, Danish and Norwegian.

## Read the results

Results appear in one block per part of speech (noun, verb, adjective and so on), with a short **Definition**. Within each block you will find up to two groups:

- **Strong** — near synonyms, with close meaning and register that you can usually swap in.
- **Weak** — looser or associated words that are related but not fully interchangeable. This group is left out when there are none.

If nothing matches you will see *No synonyms found.*

## Who can use it

You need to be signed in with a verified account, or you will see *Sign in with a verified account to use the thesaurus.*

## Usage

- **pagenorma S** — 100 free searches per month. After that, searches use your tokens, and one token covers 500 searches. If you have none, you will see *Not enough tokens are left for the thesaurus. Buy tokens or redeem a voucher to continue.*
- **pagenorma QS** — unrestricted.

See [Monthly allowance]({% link support/usage/monthly-allowance.md %}).

## From the glossary

You can also look up synonyms from the 📖 button next to a term in the [project glossary]({% link support/terminology/project-glossary.md %}).

> Results are AI-generated. Check them before relying on them in your translation.
