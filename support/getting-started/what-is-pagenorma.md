pagenorma is a browser-based workspace for literary translators. It helps you plan a translation, keep an eye on your pace and deadline, see what you will earn, and compare your translation with the original — with AI commentary, a searchable glossary and a thesaurus alongside.

You use it at [pagenorma.eu/app](https://pagenorma.eu/app/) in any modern browser. It is a progressive web app, so you can also [install it like an app]({% link support/getting-started/install-pagenorma-as-an-app.md %}).

## What you can do

- **Track projects.** Add a book, its rate and its deadline. pagenorma shows how many pages you have translated, the pace you need to keep, and what you have earned so far.
- **Forecast earnings.** Financial Analytics shows your net earnings month by month after your own deductions.
- **Review a translation.** Open a side-by-side view of source, translation and AI commentary, paragraph by paragraph.
- **Keep terminology consistent.** Browse an AI-generated glossary with quality assessments, and look up synonyms in the thesaurus.

## The main screens

Open the menu (the ☰ button on small screens) to move between screens.

| Menu item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your projects, progress, pace and earnings. Start a **New Project** here. |
| **Financial Analytics** | Monthly net earnings for the year you choose. |
| **Translation Analyses** | Your saved AI analyses and the side-by-side view. |
| **Thesaurus** | Synonyms grouped by part of speech, in 14 languages. |
| **Settings** | Deductions, Hugging Face, Google Docs and database backup. |
| **Login / Profile** | Sign in, or manage your account once you are signed in. |

## Do I need an account?

Not to track your work. Projects, the dashboard, Financial Analytics and backups work without signing in. You need a verified account for the AI features: analysis, the glossary and the thesaurus. See [Create an account]({% link support/account/create-an-account.md %}).

## Quick start

1. [Create a project]({% link support/projects/create-a-project.md %}) from your source ePub or PDF and your translation.
2. Check your pace and earnings on the [project card]({% link support/projects/understand-your-project-card.md %}).
3. Optionally [run an AI analysis]({% link support/analysis/run-an-analysis.md %}) and review the results.
4. [Back up your data]({% link support/data/back-up-and-restore.md %}) from time to time.
