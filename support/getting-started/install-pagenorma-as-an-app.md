pagenorma is a progressive web app (PWA). Installing it gives you a window of its own, an icon on your desktop or home screen, and no browser address bar.

## Install on a computer

1. Open [pagenorma.eu/app](https://pagenorma.eu/app/) in Chrome or Edge.
2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose **Install pagenorma**.
3. Confirm. pagenorma opens in its own window.

## Install on Android

1. Open the app in Chrome.
2. Open the menu and choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

## Install on iPhone or iPad

1. Open the app in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.

## What works offline

The app files are cached on your device, so pagenorma can open without a connection and you can look at the projects stored on your device.

Some things always need the internet:

- signing in, your token balance and voucher redemption
- picking or refreshing a Google Doc
- AI analysis, the glossary and the thesaurus
- some helper libraries the app loads when you read ePub and PDF files

## Updates

pagenorma checks for new versions whenever you open it and updates itself. If you ever see an old version, see [The app is not updating]({% link support/troubleshooting/app-not-updating.md %}).

> **Your projects live in your browser.** Installing the app does not move them anywhere else, and a different device or browser starts with no projects. Use [backup and restore]({% link support/data/back-up-and-restore.md %}) to move or protect your work.
