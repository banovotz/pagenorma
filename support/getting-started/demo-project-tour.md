If your workspace contains the demo project, *The Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde*, the Dashboard shows a **Demo Project** banner with a **Start Demo Tour** button. The tour is a quick way to see the main features without setting up your own book.

> The demo project is not present in every workspace. If you do not see the banner, the tour is not available for you.

## The four steps

1. **Project parameters and documents.** On the project card, the tour points to **Edit**, where you change the deadline, daily page goal and rate per standard page. To manage your own book you would upload your source ePub or PDF here and connect your translation file or a Google Doc.
2. **Translation analytics and reviews.** On **Translation Analyses**, the tour points to **Open Analysis**, which shows the paragraph-by-paragraph comparison of source and translation with AI commentary.
3. **Project glossary and terminology.** In the commentary column, the tour points to **View Glossary**, the list of key terms, names and recurring phrases that helps keep terminology consistent.
4. **Settings: Google account.** In **Settings**, the tour points to the Google connection you need for private Google Docs. pagenorma can read only the documents you choose.

You can restart the tour at any time with **Start Demo Tour**.

## Experiment freely

The demo project is a normal project. You can edit it, open its analysis and delete it when you are done, without affecting your own work.
