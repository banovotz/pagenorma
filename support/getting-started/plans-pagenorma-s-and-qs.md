pagenorma comes in two tiers. They share the same screens and project tools; what differs is how the AI features are powered and how much you can use them.

## At a glance

| | **pagenorma S** | **pagenorma QS** |
|---|---|---|
| Project tracking, analytics, backups | Yes | Yes |
| How AI is powered | Your own Hugging Face account or token | pagenorma's own AI connection |
| Hugging Face connection needed | Yes | No |
| Analysis pages | 5 free standard pages per month, then tokens | Unrestricted |
| Glossary pages | 5 free standard pages per month, then tokens | Unrestricted |
| Thesaurus searches | 100 free per month, then tokens | Unrestricted |

Every account starts on pagenorma S.

## How to tell which plan you are on

Look at the logo at the top of the Dashboard. It reads **pagenorma S** or **pagenorma QS**. The *AI analysis allowance* card on the Dashboard also tells you: on QS it says that unrestricted AI analysis, glossary generation and thesaurus are included, and on S it shows how much of your monthly allowance is left.

## Good to know

- Your plan is applied to your account by pagenorma. It does not change if you edit something in your browser.
- Without an account you can still use the project-tracking tools, but not the AI features.
- Token purchases are not available yet. See [Tokens explained]({% link support/usage/tokens-explained.md %}).
- For questions about QS access, use the **Contact** link at the bottom of the app.
