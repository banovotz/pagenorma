Financial Analytics turns your gross earnings into net earnings using the model you choose in **Settings → Financials & Deductions**.

## Choose a model

**Sole proprietorship / Business (fixed monthly contribution deductions)**

Enter **Fixed monthly contributions (€)**. This amount is subtracted once for every month in which at least one project is due. Months with no project due show €0.00.

*Example:* contributions are €200. In June two projects are due, with €1,500 and €500 gross. The June net is €1,800.

**Percentage deduction per contract (author contract / freelance)**

Enter **Contribution rate (%)**. Each project's gross amount is reduced by that percentage in the month it is due.

*Example:* the rate is 15% and a project's gross is €1,000. The net is €850.

## Save

Click **Save Settings**. You will see *Settings saved successfully!*

## Standard page

**Standard Page Definition** is set to *1800 characters with spaces*. The 1500-character option is marked as coming soon.

## Good to know

- These settings change the **Analytics** screen only. The gross totals on the Dashboard are not affected.
- They are stored in this browser. They are not part of a [database backup]({% link support/data/back-up-and-restore.md %}), so note them down if you move to another device.
- Under the fixed model, a month's net can be negative, and is then shown in red.
