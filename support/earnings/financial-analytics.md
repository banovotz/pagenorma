**Financial Analytics** (in the menu) shows what your projects earn, month by month, after your own deductions. The page notes that the figures are informational and meant for planning your pace and earnings.

## Choose a year

Use **Select year** to pick the current year, or up to two years before or after it.

## Read the table

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Month** | January to December. |
| **Translation (Title)** | Each project active in that month. |
| **Client / Publisher** | The client you entered, or *-*. |
| **Net Earnings (€)** | The net amount, shown in the month the project is due. |
| **Notes** | **On schedule**, or **⚠️ Project delayed**. |

The last row, **Total annual net earnings**, adds up the year.

## Which month does the money count in?

- A project is listed in every month between its **Start Date** and its **Target Deadline**.
- Its earnings count only in the month of its **deadline**. In its other months the cell says *n/a*.
- A month with no active projects says *No active projects* and shows €0.00.

## How the gross amount is calculated

The gross amount is your translation in standard pages (characters ÷ 1800) multiplied by the project's rate per page. If a project has no translation text yet, its total page count is used instead.

## What "delayed" means

A project is marked **⚠️ Project delayed** when its deadline has passed and not all pages are translated yet.

## Net amounts

Net amounts depend on the deduction model in Settings. See [Set up your deductions]({% link support/earnings/set-up-deductions.md %}). The deductions affect only this screen. The totals on the Dashboard stay gross.
