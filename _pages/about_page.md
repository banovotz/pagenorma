# Built at the Translation Desk

Managing multiple book translation projects across different publishing houses quickly reveals gaps in standard software available to literary translators. Commercial CAT tools treat long-form literature like technical manuals, while keeping track of deadlines and daily targets often devolved into endless manual calculations. 

**pagenorma** was created directly at the translation desk to cut through that daily hassle and give literary translators complete control over their workflow, finances, and prose consistency.

---

## What Makes pagenorma Different?

### 1. Dynamic Daily Pacing
Instead of static schedules that break the moment life happens, pagenorma reads progress directly from your active document—whether you write in **Microsoft Word** or **Google Docs**. It compares your current word, character, or standard page count against your source file (ePub, PDF) and project deadline, giving you a real-time, dynamic daily target to keep you comfortably on track.

### 2. Income & Financial Insights
Freelance literary translation requires clear financial foresight. pagenorma translates your daily character or word targets into real-time earnings forecasts for every active project, helping you track monthly revenue as you translate.

### 3. Deep Narrative Consistency & AI-Powered QA
Long-form prose demands meticulous stylistic and narrative continuity. pagenorma automatically extracts a glossary and concordance index directly from your translation as you work. With searchable side-by-side views, inline commentary, and AI-powered QA, verifying key terminology, recurring idioms, and voice consistency has never been smoother.

---

## Local First & Privacy Focused

We believe your translated manuscripts and proprietary terminology belong strictly to you. pagenorma operates as a Progressive Web Application (PWA) with a local-first philosophy:

* **Your data stays with you:** Progress tracking and text analysis happen locally in your browser workspace.
* **No model training:** Your manuscripts are never harvested, rented, or used to train third-party machine learning models.
* **Offline capability:** Access your pacing tools and work dashboard even when you are working offline.
