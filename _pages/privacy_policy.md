*Last updated: September 24, 2026*

At **pagenorma**, we believe that the stories and texts you translate belong entirely to you. Because pagenorma is designed specifically for book translators working across dense long-form prose, privacy and data control are foundational to how the app is built.

## 1. Data Collection and Local Storage
* **Client-Side First:** pagenorma operates primarily within your browser and local environment. Your source texts, translated documents, glossaries, and concordance indexes remain on your device or are handled directly through your active workspace sessions.
* **Minimal Telemetry:** We do not track, read, or store the contents of your book manuscripts, translations, or proprietary terminology lists on external servers. 

## 2. Document Integrations (Word and Google Docs)
When you connect pagenorma to your live workflow files (such as Word or Google Docs):
* The application reads character counts, word counts, and progress markers strictly to calculate your **dynamic daily targets** and **real-time pace forecasts**.

## 3. AI-Powered QA and Textual Analysis
When utilizing built-in concordance, glossary extraction, and AI-powered QA features:
* Data processing is handled securely and transiently to provide you with inline commentary and side-by-side verification.
* Your proprietary texts are never retained beyond the scope of your active analytical session.

## 4. Subscription and Account Data 
If you subscribe to **pagenorma QS** or register for an account:
* We collect only the essential billing and contact details required to process your subscription securely through our trusted payment processors.
* We never share, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product enhancements or legal updates. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with a revised update date.
